Pizza

Pizza is a globally popular Italian dish originating from Naples, consisting of a flattened bread dough base traditionally topped with tomatoes, mozzarella, olive oil, and herbs. Emerging in the 18th century as quick, affordable food for the working class, it has evolved into a diverse, versatile meal with countless regional variations and toppings.

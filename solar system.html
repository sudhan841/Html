Our solar system features eight distinct planets orbiting the Sun, divided into rocky inner planets and outer gas/ice giants, all orbiting on the same plane. Mercury is the smallest and hottest/coldest (no atmosphere), Venus is the hottest (thick atmosphere), Earth supports life, and Mars has giant volcanoes. The giants include Jupiter (largest), Saturn (rings), Uranus (sideways rotation), and Neptune (fastest winds).
